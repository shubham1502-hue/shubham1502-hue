# Shubham Singh

I turn business requirements into workflows that teams can run and measure. My work spans business operations, revenue operations, and SaaS implementation, with a focus on how data moves between teams, how exceptions get resolved, and how leaders decide what to fix first.

## Professional experience

**Datavio.** I configured and supported workflows that turned marketplace purchase orders into sales orders in SAP Business One and ERPNext. My work covered REST API testing, payload validation, SKU and warehouse mapping, and troubleshooting with clients and engineers. I also worked on document extraction, reconciliation, and replenishment planning.

## Product experiments

I use ThoroughLoop to explore whether a short, repeatable review helps founders move from scattered context to a decision they can follow up on. The [live demo](https://thoroughloop.vercel.app) uses rule-based diagnosis and includes fictional sample workflows.
